Ensign Deadman was a crewman on the *[Hanesawa](/ships/hanesawa)*. 

During the [Crisis on Cressida](/mission/cressida3/) who piloted the evacuation shuttle hijacked by Klingons. He was [freed from his restraints](/mission/cressida4/) and then safely piloted the shuttle back to the ship. 

He was a member of the away team visiting the strange copy of the *Hanesawa* during the [Nest in the Dark](/mission/nest2) mission. As part of a test of the crew he was trapped within an airtight bubble as they raced to solve a puzzle, releasing him with seconds to spare.
