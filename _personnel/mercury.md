Mercury is the name settled on by the universal translator after this being appeared in front of the away team [during their visit to the copy](/mission/nest2) of the *[Hanesawa](/ships/hanesawa)* within the Matroyshka Brain headed towards the Foggy Bottom system. Mercury was a two and a half meter tall humanoid, pale, naked, sexless, and nearly featureless except for two expressive human-like blue eyes and a well-formed mouth, and spoke in Federation Standard to welcome the crew.
