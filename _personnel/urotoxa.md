An aging and distinguished Nausicaan woman, Urotoxa leads an archaeological expedition that has been studying the native Kizomic people and their technology. Holding the Nausicaan equivalent of advanced degrees in ancient technology, palynology, and computer sciences, Urotoxa is a shrewd thinker and an opportunistic strategist. She has spoken passionately in lectures across the sector, and she has used her oratorical skills to gather funding for numerous expeditions in the name of scientific advancement. 

During [the evacuation of Cressida](/mission/cressida3), she pleaded with Captain Ral and the crew of the *Hanesawa* to deliver weapons to the native Kizomans to use in defending themselves against the attacking trimetric rift creatures. The Captain declined, citing the Prime Directive. Lt. Ania Hagen discovered that she truly wanted to help the Kizomans but she also did not want the crew to visit the planet and discover something she was hiding.

After being rebuffed by the Captain, a group of Klingons that had been evacuating were given the impression that a glorious battle was taking place on the planet below, leading them to [hijack an evacuation shuttle](/mission/cressida3) to go join the fight. Urotoxa denies any direct involvement.

Once the creature attacking the native Kizomic city [was dealt with](/mission/cressida4), a representative of the natives told Captain Ral that she had been visiting the city often, bringing supplies in exchange for being left alone while she worked with an unkown device, which the natives initially feared was the cause of the creature's attack, though they now believe Urotoxa saved them by bringing the crew down to stop the creature.
