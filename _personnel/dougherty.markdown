Admiral Dougherty was a Rear Admiral managing Starfleet Alpha Quadrant logistics, one of four in command of the division. In 2371, he [expanded his fleet](/mission/prelude) with eight new Excelsior class commissions for eight new captains, including [Yeldrin Ral](/personnel/ral).

After the [Crisis on Cressida](/mission/cressida5/) mission, he alerted Captain Ral that [Urotoxa](/personnel/urotoxa) had been released from custody as she was not a Federation citizen and thus had not committed any crimes despite violating the Prime Directive, warning him that potential future run-ins with either her or [Administrator Damaad](/personnel/damaad) may be dangerous.
